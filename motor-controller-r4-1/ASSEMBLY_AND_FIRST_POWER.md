# R4.1 assembly and commissioning

1. Match this revision's native files, Gerbers, quote inputs and locked UF2. The old R4 five-driver firmware is incompatible with leaving U4/U5 empty. Do not order from an older cart or quote.
2. The active budget route has no factory fitting. Follow BUY_AND_SOLDER_MANUAL.md for U7/U8 and every other joint. Obtain experienced hands-on help for the small chips if you cannot solder and inspect them reliably. The TOP2 factory files are an alternative only.
3. Before soldering, inspect the bare/partly assembled board for damage, bridges and open joints. Do not bridge a fuse. Fit F1/F2 and rear D2 with correct ratings/polarity; fit the through-hole parts,10 female 8-pin socket rows and vertical5.08 mm motor screw terminals. Never replace phase terminals with unrated signal headers.
4. Before mounting it, program the genuine bare RP2040-Zero with 23 manually added header pins with the supplied `firmware/Seizo_R41_THREE_DRIVER_LOCKED_BENCH.uf2`: hold BOOT, connect USB, release BOOT and copy the UF2 to its boot drive. Disconnect USB afterward. Fit the exact module via its 23 header pins; it is directly soldered, not independently socketed. Connect J9.1 to the verified Zero Key2.1 RUN net and J9.2 to Key2.2 GND. RUN is not an edge-header pin. Identify the RESET/Key2 contacts using the official module schematic and a working multimeter; do not guess switch feet by appearance. Complete the documented dry-contact HOLD loop without external voltage.
5. Inspect every solder joint under magnification and use a working meter for continuity/polarity before any supply. Fix bridges/opens while fully unpowered. A damaged PCB or failed isolation/current path is a stop condition; do not rely on software to make bad soldering safe. Use an experienced helper for the small SOT23-6part or other joints beyond available tools/skill.
6. Verify the owned regulated12 V adapter label, plug fit, center polarity and unloaded/loaded voltage.12V-only design; do not try24V. The initial shared input qualification envelope is at most2.5A/30W, not an enforced current clamp or a certified continuous rating. A4 A fuse does not establish that operating envelope.
7. Keep motors and driver modules absent for initial power/logic/harness checks. Use a suitable current-limited bench source or a qualified test arrangement. With the J9 harness connected and motor power absent, the MCU is intentionally held in reset: USB serial will not enumerate even if USB supplies the 3.3 V rail. Normal serial operation needs checked motor power. Verify no short, expected supply rails, reset/HOLD operation and USB enumeration without heating. No physical first-power test has been performed by this release process.
8. With every power source disconnected, inspect the exact delivered BTT TMC2209 V1.3/R110 modules, pinout/shunts, heatsinks and insulation; fit only U1/U2/U3. U4/U5 must remain absent. Keep the delivered movement-locked UF2. The reference CHECK must verify populated mask 7; delivered ARM/MOVE remain locked.
9. Connect only a qualified motor after identifying its two coil pairs by unpowered resistance and matching the selected motor/driver winding-current limit. Coil order is A1, A2, B1, B2. Do not hot-plug motors or modules. Start with an unloaded secured single-axis bench mechanism, not a suspended arm. Actual currents, torque, cooling, diode/socket/fuse temperature, USB/UART, supply dips and regenerative transients need instrumented tests before progressing.
10. The current planned settings request about707/707/354 mA RMS (rounded down to704/704/337 mA). They are not proven suitable for arbitrary NEMA 23/NEMA 17 motors. Stronger motors may need stronger external drivers and a different supply; do not raise limits to solve weak torque without requalification.

HOLD is a software/logic control, not an independent safety-rated stop or brake. Reset/power loss may release torque and drop the arm. Secure/counterbalance gravity loads and keep people clear.

Fallback: the active manual plan leaves four extra bare PCBs. Drivers are replaceable via sockets, but MCU and many passive parts are soldered. The unchanged existing CNC/Uno can still operate its original three axes separately. An Uno CNCshield is not a drop-in controller for all five new slots. No full assembled backup is included in this budget.


## Power budget scope

The reused 12 V/4 A adapter is allocated to the **new carrier plan**. The existing CNC shield also needs motor power. This BOM does **not** establish that one adapter can safely power the combined six-motor arm, and actual motor winding currents are unknown. Concurrent CNC-plus-carrier operation, shared adapter wiring and combined peak/continuous demand are unqualified. A separate appropriately sized supply and rated distribution may be necessary and would cost extra. Do not parallel adapter outputs. This is a carrier-component budget, not a guaranteed complete new-arm motor/power budget.
